### **Cardend**  
- **Location**: Cardend is a small, rustic village situated at the foot of the *Grimholt Mountains*, surrounded by dense forests and rolling hills. The village is known for its quiet charm, nestled in a valley near a river that winds through the landscape. It is a secluded and tranquil place, with only a few main roads connecting it to the outside world. The village’s economy has historically relied on agriculture, with grain being one of its main crops.  

- **Population**: Roughly 200-300 people, mostly farmers, traders, and artisans who make their living off the land or the river. While Cardend is small, it is self-sufficient, and its inhabitants have a strong sense of community.  

- **Economy & Industry**: The introduction of Gerard’s affordable windmill revolutionized the village's farming practices. Unlike the larger, more expensive water-powered mills, these windmills allowed farmers to mill their grain on-site, saving time and effort. This innovation boosted the local economy, making Cardend a small but significant player in regional trade.  

- **Village Features**:  
   - **Main Square & Market**: The village’s center is a humble but busy market square, where locals gather to trade grain, vegetables, and goods. Gerard's shop is located here, where he sells his windmills, tools, and inventions.

### **Key Characters**:

1. **Gerard** – *The Merchant Inventor*  
   - **Background**: Gerard is a visionary who moved to Cardend after the landslide and became the village's most influential merchant. In his 40s, he is ambitious and highly resourceful, having invented the small, affordable windmill kit that has allowed local farmers to process their grain near their fields.  
   - **Personality**: He’s a forward-thinking and determined man, with a penchant for practical solutions and a deep love for innovation. Though he’s well-liked for his invention, he’s also seen by some as someone who benefits from the hardship of others — especially since the windmill was a direct response to the failure of the old mill in Mill Hollow.  
   - **Conflict**: Gerard’s success has brought prosperity to Cardend, but it has also drawn attention. Some people suspect that the windmill was only possible because of the failure of Mill Hollow, and the tensions with the past — particularly the loss of the mill and the damming of the river — are starting to surface in ways that Gerard may not fully understand. His son, Marcello, was deeply troubled by these tensions, and his disappearance may be tied to these unresolved issues.

2. **Marcello** – *The Disappeared Son*  
   - **Background**: Gerard’s only son, Marcello, is in his early 20s and has always felt overshadowed by his father’s success. A curious and adventurous young man, he often found the village’s quiet life stifling and looked to Mill Hollow and the old ruins for something more exciting.  
   - **Personality**: Marcello was headstrong, skeptical of his father’s methods, and eager to prove himself. He wasn’t interested in continuing the family business of inventing or managing windmills, and he often clashed with Gerard over the future of the family’s fortune.  
   - **Conflict**: Marcello’s disappearance came after he made a trip to Mill Hollow, and he hasn’t been heard from since. While some believe he went there to explore the old ruins or look for treasure, others suspect he might have had darker reasons — perhaps to confront the past, or even sabotage the success of his father’s windmill empire. Was he simply seeking freedom, or did he uncover something dangerous in the ruins of Mill Hollow?

3. **Tessa** – *The Suspicious Servant*  
   - **Background**: Tessa has served Gerard’s family for many years, working in their household and tending to their needs. She’s practical, observant, and knows the inner workings of the family better than anyone. She’s grown particularly close to Marcello, having watched him grow up, and is deeply concerned by his sudden disappearance.  
   - **Personality**: Tessa is cautious, intuitive, and protective of the family. She’s also a bit skeptical of Gerard’s blind optimism about the future of Cardend, especially given the shadows of Mill Hollow’s collapse. She suspects that there may be more to Marcello’s disappearance than just youthful recklessness.  
   - **Conflict**: Tessa fears that Marcello’s trip to Mill Hollow was not one of innocent exploration, but that he might have been involved in something more sinister. She is convinced that something in the ruins — or perhaps in the old mill itself — has a dark hold over those who venture near it. She is determined to find out the truth about what happened to Marcello, even if it means going into the heart of the ruined village herself.
