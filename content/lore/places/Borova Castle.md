Borova Castle is built at the top of the waterfall where the [[Great River]] falls from the [[Great Northern mountains]]. There are massive water wheels used to power lifts to raise and lower goods to keep the castle supplied. 

The castle is home to the [[Knights of Borova]] and was a bastion defending the [[World]] from the monsters in the north.

[[Lord Kaypon]] is the lord of the castle.
