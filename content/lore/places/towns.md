2. **Havennor** (to the west, near the foothills of Maltova)  
   - A small, rugged town at the border between Veldor and Maltova, Havennor thrives on wool production and pastoral trade, particularly from the hardy sheep raised in the nearby highlands. The town is surrounded by smaller settlements and nomadic herders who winter in the foothills.

3. **Velde** (to the northeast, on the plains)  
   - A larger, well-established town located along the primary trade route running through the plains. Velde is known for its vast cattle ranches and horse breeding. It has strong connections to both Cardend and the larger cities of Brixia, and it plays an important role in the region’s economy.

4. **Pellin** (to the south, near the foothills)  
   - A small, fortified settlement located where the plains meet the edge of the *Grimholt Mountains*. Pellin is known for its defensive fortifications, as it serves as a guard post for travelers moving between Veldor and Maltova. It is also a gateway town for those exploring the mountainous regions to the north.

These towns and cities form a loose network that connects the more remote Cardend to the bustling trade routes of Brixia, while also serving as waypoints for travelers heading toward the pastoral expanses of Maltova.
